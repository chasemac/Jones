Design system

Inter + Space Grotesk

UI uses Inter for density and readability. Space Grotesk handles numerics, labels, and headings — quietly graphic without going retro.

Color

Coral, teal, warm off-white

Backgrounds are warm off-white (#FAF7F2) with soft borders. Coral drives the primary CTA, teal handles money, red is reserved for debt and warnings.

Privacy (GDD §15)

Only you, on your turn

Other players never show up as tokens on the board. The Joneses rival is public (they're the antagonist). Cash/debt/meters are hidden during handoff.

06 · Rotate prompt iOS Safari fallback

Rotate for more room

The Express Lane looks best in landscape. Turn your phone sideways — or unlock rotation if locked.

Layout

Stretched ring keeps travel intuitive

The 12 shops sit on a wide ellipse — visually a continuous loop with a dashed road and clockwise chevrons. Adjacent shops are adjacent on screen, so "Coffee Shop → Megamart is one hop" reads at a glance, instead of jumping rows.

Landscape on the web

No way to force iOS Safari

Android PWAs can request landscape via the manifest. iOS Safari ignores it. The pattern: detect portrait with matchMedia and show a rotate prompt that also nudges users to unlock rotation.

Action strip

Context-first, not inventory-first

The middle band pairs the current location card with up to three primary actions. Anything rarer (inventory, goals) lives in floating glyphs on the edge — no HUD column eating 30% of the width.
