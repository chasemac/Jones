Scannability

Five seconds to read the week

Ledger totals, balance sheet, needs, goals — every block has a header, a signature number in Space Grotesk, and a delta. No paragraphs of prose. The Continue button is always in the same spot.

Queue indicator

Modals stack, never double-render

A small pill in the stage corner tells the player how many events are queued. News, warnings, and the week summary run one at a time so nothing gets dismissed accidentally.

Warnings are actionable

Don't just warn — offer a fix

Low-hunger warnings list the three cheapest/fastest options with cost and travel distance. The primary CTA routes the player straight there instead of dumping them back on the board to figure it out.
