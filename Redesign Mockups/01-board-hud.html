Signature layout, modern skin

Shops on the ring, action in the middle

The 12 storefronts stay on a ring like the original Jones in the Fast Lane. The central stage is where you actually do things — the current location's actions appear there with their cost/outcome inline, so no button is a blind click.

Hot-seat privacy (GDD §15)

One active token, one HUD

Only Alex's token renders on the board; the Joneses always show as the public rival. Cash, debt, and needs live in the left sidebar for the active player only — the handoff screen wipes them between turns.

Scannability

Numbers in Space Grotesk, UI in Inter

Money, costs, weeks, and meters render in Space Grotesk so you can eyeball changes fast. Body labels stay in Inter to avoid display-font fatigue during long sessions.
